# CPC legacy data removed

At the user's request, downloaded CPC legacy tables, extracted Connecticut records,
and temporary copies were deleted. The downloader and old menu view were removed.
The removal inventory is in audit/cpc-cmi-removal.json; it contains no data values.
No CPC CMI data is used for training or scoring.

The replacement is the separate root-zone soil moisture experiment described in
ROOT-ZONE-SOIL-MOISTURE.md. It is not connected to an overall drought model.
